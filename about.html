About

From making machines move to asking how people live with them.

My path has moved from robotics, to psychology and relationships, and finally toward human factors: the discipline that lets me connect human evidence with physical and intelligent systems.

Download CVContact me

The through line

My subject changed. My habit of asking why a system behaves as it does did not.

Elementary and middle school

Machines first

I assembled and programmed LEGO Mindstorms EV3 and VEX robots for soccer, line-following, and task-completion competitions. I focused on components, instructions, and failure: what would make the machine perform reliably?

High school

Attention moved toward people

Growing up in an exam-oriented education system and living through COVID-era family confinement made behavior feel newly observable. I became interested in how people infer intentions and emotions from subtle changes. Psychology gave me language and methods for that curiosity.

University

Relationships became research

My early questions concerned how people adapt when stable assumptions and relationships become uncertain. Training and research in Functional Analytic Psychotherapy and Awareness, Courage & Love helped me examine interpersonal behavior as something that can be defined, observed, and studied.

Current direction

People and systems together

Research, internship experiences, and independent projects showed me that interfaces and AI systems also shape uncertainty, trust, disclosure, and attachment. I now want to design and evaluate human-machine systems with psychological rigor and engineering accountability.

What I bring

Behavioral depth with a builder's orientation.

I am most useful where psychological constructs must become workflows, measures, requirements, prototypes, and ethical boundaries.

Behavioral research

Experimental design, participant-facing research, psychometrics, surveys, behavioral coding, transcript preparation, and quantitative analysis.

Product design

User journeys, task decomposition, interaction design, prototyping, usability-test planning, and literature-to-requirement translation.

Responsible AI

Human oversight, uncertainty communication, memory control, privacy, emotional dependency, labeling assumptions, and system accountability.

Direction

Human factors for AI-enabled and embodied systems.

My professional goal is to work as a human factors engineer and human-centered product designer, following a problem from behavioral evidence through requirements, implementation, risk analysis, and evaluation.
