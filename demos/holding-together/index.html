Holding Together

Stay in control of a conversation and an embrace.

This walkthrough simulates call and touch permissions. It sends no audio, requests no microphone access and operates no hardware. Both partner roles are represented on this page.

    Walkthrough tasks

    1. Accept a call. Explain whether touch is enabled.
    2. Decline touch while staying on the call.
    3. Accept touch, send a gesture, then mute. Explain which channel changed.
    4. Stop touch while continuing the call.
    5. Hang up or disconnect during a gesture. Verify that touch and microphone permission both end.

    The 3-second gesture duration belongs only to this simulation. It is not a hug recommendation. The planned real-call prototype is a separate engineering stage.
