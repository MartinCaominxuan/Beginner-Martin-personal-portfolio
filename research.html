Publications and presentations

Written and spoken research.

Published · September 30, 2026

From Isolation to Connection

A Trauma-Informed Awareness, Courage & Love Pilot Intervention for Adults with Adverse Childhood Experiences

This four-session, single-arm pilot examined the feasibility and preliminary outcomes of a connection-focused intervention for adults with histories of childhood adversity. Participants described growing trust, comfort with vulnerability, and experiences of being seen and supported. Because four participants contributed sufficient data for final analyses, the findings are preliminary and do not establish efficacy or causality.

Medical Research Archives · 14(9)Fourth author
