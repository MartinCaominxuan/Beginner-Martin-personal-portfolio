# Beginner Martin — Psychology, HCI, and Human-Centered AI Portfolio

This repository is a professional portfolio for projects at the intersection of psychology, human-computer interaction, UX research, cognitive psychology, emotion recognition, and responsible AI for mental health. It is designed to support both graduate school applications and industry-facing portfolio review.

## Portfolio Goals

- Demonstrate research readiness through literature reviews, study designs, and reproducible project documentation.
- Showcase applied UX and HCI work through case studies, prototypes, research plans, and evaluation reports.
- Present human-centered AI projects that connect psychological theory with ethical, usable, and inclusive technology.
- Maintain a clean structure that makes it easy for faculty, admissions committees, recruiters, and collaborators to review my work.

## Recommended Project Types

### Human-Centered AI

- AI explainability evaluation: compare how different explanation formats affect user trust and understanding.
- Human-AI decision-making study: test when people rely on or override AI recommendations.
- Bias and fairness audit: evaluate a public model or dataset for demographic, cultural, or accessibility concerns.

### HCI and UX Research

- Usability study of a mental health, education, or productivity app.
- Interview-based study on how students use AI tools for learning or emotional support.
- Survey project measuring user trust, perceived usefulness, perceived risk, or cognitive load.
- Prototype redesign with personas, user journeys, wireframes, and evaluation findings.

### Cognitive Psychology

- Attention and notification design experiment.
- Memory and interface layout analysis.
- Cognitive load comparison between two app flows.
- Decision-making study about automation bias or recommendation systems.

### Emotion Recognition and Affective Computing

- Literature review on emotion recognition validity and cultural bias.
- Small classification project using a public dataset, with careful discussion of limitations.
- Annotation study showing disagreement in emotion labels and implications for AI design.
- Multimodal emotion research proposal combining text, facial expression, voice, or physiological signals.

### AI and Mental Health

- Ethical design review of AI mental health chatbots.
- Safety-focused prototype for mood tracking, journaling, or help-seeking support.
- Literature review on AI mental health interventions and risks.
- Research proposal on user trust, crisis escalation, privacy, or emotional dependency in AI support tools.

## Suggested Project README Template

Each project folder should include a `README.md` with the following sections:

```markdown
# Project Title

## Overview
Briefly describe the project and why it matters.

## Research Question
State the main question or design problem.

## Motivation
Explain the psychology, HCI, UX, or AI context.

## Methods
Describe participants, materials, dataset, procedure, measures, tools, or analysis plan.

## Results or Expected Outcomes
Summarize findings, prototypes, visuals, or expected contributions.

## Ethical Considerations
Discuss consent, privacy, bias, accessibility, safety, and limitations.

## Skills Demonstrated
List research, technical, design, statistical, and communication skills.

## Files
Explain the important files in the project folder.

## Reflection and Next Steps
Describe what you learned and how the work could improve.
```

## Quality Standards

- Use clear project titles and concise summaries.
- Include visuals such as diagrams, charts, prototype screenshots, and study flows when possible.
- Document methods well enough that another person could understand or reproduce the work.
- Discuss limitations honestly, especially for AI, emotion recognition, and mental health topics.
- Avoid sharing private participant data, identifiable information, or copyrighted materials without permission.
- Prefer small, polished projects over many unfinished projects.
