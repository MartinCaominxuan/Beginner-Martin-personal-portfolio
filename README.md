Admissions-facing portfolio for work at the intersection of psychology, human factors,
human–AI interaction, embodied interaction, and responsible product design.

## Selected case studies

- **Waiting With You:** a runnable AI-assisted recruitment prototype reframed around
  candidate waiting, transparent status, and responsible use of behavioral signals.
- **IP Alive:** an exploratory physical–digital character platform addressing persistent
  interaction, user-controlled memory, attachment, monetization, and character governance.
- **Pause With You:** a design-oriented focused review and speculative physical product
  concept translating smoking ritual into task analysis, safety constraints, and a staged
  validation plan.

## Accuracy and privacy

- Prototype features are distinguished from production integrations.
- Exploratory survey evidence is not presented as product validation.
- Pause With You makes no safety, cessation, or clinical-effectiveness claim.
- No identifiable participant data or confidential research data are published.
- The ACL pilot article is listed as published in *Medical Research Archives*, 14(9), with its DOI and appropriately cautious interpretation of the preliminary findings.
